Lavender

Lavandula, from the mint family

A small Mediterranean shrub with grey leaves and purple flower spikes. It loves sun, needs little water, and the bees visit it all summer.

    How to grow it

    1. Sun. Plant it in the sunniest spot you have.
    2. Soil. Use light, well-drained soil. Wet roots are its biggest enemy.
    3. Water. Water young plants; older plants rarely need it.
    4. Prune. Cut it back after flowering, but never into the old, bare wood.

    What to use it for

    In the kitchen

    A few flowers of English lavender flavour biscuits, honey and lemonade. Use very little.

    In the house

    Dry bunches upside down in a dark place, then put them in small bags in the wardrobe.

    For the bees

    Bees and butterflies love it. Leave some flowers on the plant for them.
